About Me

My journey, the work I enjoy, and a bit about who I am.

I'm Sami, a first-year ICT student at Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP) in Dhaka. I'm the kind of person who learns best by building: if I'm curious about something, I open the editor and make it.

My programming journey. It began with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, where I fell in love with seeing an idea appear on screen within minutes. From there I moved to React, Next.js and Tailwind CSS, and in the last few months I've built and deployed four projects on Vercel, from a workout library to a Bangla grocery-price tracker. Alongside my university courses (including Structured Programming) I'm steadily growing from a frontend builder into a full stack developer.

The work I enjoy. Turning a design into a clean, responsive interface; connecting it to real data through APIs; and polishing the small details that make an app feel good to use. I also love automating repetitive tasks and exploring how AI tools can speed up development.

Beyond code. I create content under my own brand, PromptForge, about AI tools, automation and productivity. I enjoy experimenting with new tools, making videos, and following tech trends. I'm also fascinated by electronics, and my university lab work in circuit simulation shows me how hardware and software meet.

My personality. Curious, disciplined and a team player. I ask questions, I iterate fast, and I don't stop until it works and looks right.
